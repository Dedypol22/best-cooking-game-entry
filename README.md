A timed warung-kitchen cooking game. You are the chef of a small kitchen: grab
ingredients from the pantry, chop them on the board, fry them on the stove or boil
them in the pot, assemble six recipes — Nasi Goreng, Mie Goreng, Sate Ayam, Telur
Dadar, Sup Ayam and Soto Ayam — on the plating counter, and serve each dish at the window
before its order ticket's patience runs out. Serve on time to build a streak
(x2 at 3, x3 at 5); let three orders expire and the shift ends. One 180-second
shift, no chores, just route-planning under pressure: chop while something fries,
mind the burn timer, and never serve a mystery mush.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Or build the hand-in bundle:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
